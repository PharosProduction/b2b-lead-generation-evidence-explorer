Separate four delivery requirements before comparing fees

A contact file gives you data. A campaign adds execution. Qualification requires a decision about a response, while meeting handover assigns the next sales action. A proposal can include several stages; write the acceptance rule for each purchased stage.

When a proposal is still incomplete

If a quotation promises meetings but leaves reply qualification with your team, assign that review task before treating the meeting line as a complete managed service. If your team only needs a researched contact file, specify its acceptance sample and exclude campaign execution from that comparison.

An empty field is a question to resolve. Filling every field does not verify the answers, approve a provider or authorize outreach.

Observation method and limits

The reference uses a bounded set of ten real providers with public B2B lead generation or sales development descriptions. The service statements are concise authored summaries of provider descriptions, not independent findings about delivery quality.

Source material was reviewed on November 22, 2026. Nine official pages have retained same-day captures. Direct access to the SalesRoads homepage returned HTTP 403; its official program-comparison page was read separately. The source labels and full captures remain in the private editorial archive. Competitor source links are omitted from this public reference.

No prices, conversion rates, client outcomes or provider scores were measured. The selection is not an exhaustive list of the market. Ask the provider to confirm the current scope for your engagement.
